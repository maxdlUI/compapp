# Repline Coach: the owner's handbook

The coach turns what a player writes ("45 min, my box fights are trash, cup tomorrow") into a routine built from the catalog sets. It runs on the player's device, so it costs nothing. Draft to test: https://claude.ai/artifact/Sqqk2w4naZMPceeExTG8cC

Everything below comes from the owner's answers (16 Nov 2026). Where it says "assumption", the owner hasn't decided yet.

## Who gets it

- Free: no coach, only a preview that points to the plans.
- Standard: 1 coach routine a day. Pro: 3 a day. Counted on the server.
- Launch month: everyone signed in counts as Pro.

## Player profile

- Platform: PC or console (console and "no KovaaK's" drop the KovaaK's sets).
- Controller and mouse/keyboard get the same routines.
- Levels (by what the player reached in comp, not by rank):
  - Not Unreal yet: basics, more mechanics, no scrims.
  - Unreal: first cups, fights under pressure. Scrims allowed.
  - Cup grinder: cash and Div cups every week, no earnings yet.
  - Earner: earns in cups or plays FNCS semis and finals.
- Main goal: all-round, cups, aim, mechanics.

## Time

- 1 hour with no wish: evenly split, 15 min each aim, mechanics, fights, ranked.
- Under 35 min: aim, mechanics and fights only. Over 75 min: 10 % review. Longer sessions get more live time (assumption for the exact shares).
- Drill sets last at most 15 min. Longer blocks become several sets; live sets (ranked, scrims) can be longer.
- A 5 min break after every hour.
- Order: aim → mechanics → fights → live → review (fix drill right after the VOD review).

## Weaknesses → sets (in order of importance)

| The player says | Sets that get more time |
|---|---|
| Edits | Edit wall, Freebuild 90% |
| Box fights | Turtle Fights, Piece control, Edit wall |
| Endgame | Turtle Fights, with the tip: worse subregion (higher ping) or lower FPS so it's more realistic |
| Aim | KovaaK's Tjino Tracking, KovaaK's Tjino Updated, V5 Reaction flick, 1v1 Build Fights |
| Piece control | Piece control, Turtle Fights, Freebuild 110–120% |
| Building too slow (90s, retakes, high ground) | Freebuild 110–120%, Freebuild 90%, 1v1 Build Fights |
| Nerves, choking | More ranked with one focus per game, Clutch Realistics |

## Special days

- Cup today: warm-up only, up to 60 min: aim, edit wall, fast freebuild, piece control, build fights, cup check. Nothing new.
- Cup tomorrow: normal training plus a 5 min cup check at the end.
- 7+ days in a row: one easy day a week (shorter, calmer drills, fewer live games).

## Over time

- Harder instead of longer: the same time, harder versions (Freebuild 90% → 110–120%, Piece control → Turtle → Clutch).
- After every routine the coach asks 2 questions: "Too easy / just right / too hard?" and "What killed you most today?". The next routine uses both (too hard: calmer drills and fewer live games; too easy: harder drills and more live; the cause of death gets extra time).
