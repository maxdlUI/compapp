Questions and answers.

What is the Daily Pro Routine?

Training in a fixed order. Every block has a duration or a kill target, plus the codes you need for it.

Is the routine the same for everyone?

Yes. The routines are set in advance and identical for every player. A routine made just for you will come with AI Training.

Do I need an account?

No. Every routine works without an account. With a Discord account you get your own referral link and your settings in one place.

What happens if I stop halfway?

Your progress for today is saved in this browser. On the home screen, “Continue” picks up where you left off.

Which keys can I use?

Space starts and pauses, R restarts the block, N goes to the next block, F toggles full screen.
