Questions and answers.

What is the Daily Pro Routine?

Training in a fixed order, built from how pros practise. Every set has one focus.

What is a set?

One part of your routine: a map, a mode or a review, with a time or a kill target.

Do I need an account?

To train, yes: sign in with Discord, it's free. You can look at every routine without one.

Do I stay signed in?

Yes, on this device until you sign out. On iPhone, add Repline to your Home Screen: Safari clears website data after 7 days without a visit.

Where do I get the Windows app?

On our Discord server, in #download. Free, for Windows 10 and 11.

Why is there a pause between sets?

Time to open the next map. 30 seconds by default, the set then starts on its own. Change it in Settings.

Where does today's focus come from?

From your last replay review. Focus drills and ranked games use it.

Which keys can I use?

Space start/pause · R restart (+15 s while you switch) · N next · F full screen.
