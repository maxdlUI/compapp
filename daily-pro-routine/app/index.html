Questions and answers.

What is the Daily Pro Routine?

Training in a fixed order, built from how pros practise. Every set has one focus.

What is a set?

One part of your routine: a map, a mode or a review, with a time or a kill target.

When does a routine count?

When you really trained it. Sets can't be skipped, and our server checks that the real time has passed. In the Windows app, Fortnite or KovaaK's also has to run during the game sets: then your routine is Verified and its spin gives 2 Golden Tickets. A routine that doesn't count keeps your streak, but gives no spin and no points.

Can the Windows app get me banned?

No. Repline never touches Fortnite: no memory, no game files, no network. It only looks at the list of running programs, like Task Manager or Discord does. For KovaaK's it reads the result files KovaaK's saves itself. Both can be turned off in Settings.

Do I need an account?

To train, yes: sign in with Discord, it's free. You can look at every routine without one.

Do I stay signed in?

Yes, on this device until you sign out. On iPhone, add Repline to your Home Screen: Safari clears website data after 7 days without a visit.

Where do I get the Windows app?

Right here: Download for Windows, free, for Windows 10 and 11. Run the installer once, after that Repline updates itself. If Windows warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

Why is there a pause between sets?

Time to open the next map. 30 seconds by default, the set then starts on its own. Change it in Settings.

Where does today's focus come from?

From your last replay review. Focus drills and ranked games use it.

Which keys can I use?

Space start/pause · R restart (+15 s while you switch) · N next · F full screen.

        Keys that keep it on (moving)
        Repline listens for keys like push-to-talk does. It never blocks a key, and nothing is saved or sent. Shooting and aiming (left and right click) never turn it off.

        How it works
        1. Switch on the tools you want. They start with your next routine and stop when it ends.
        2. Focus Cue: one focus as an if-then plan. Your cue word comes at random moments. After a fight: did it or forgot. 80 % on 3 days and it's Locked in.
        3. Focus Tunnel: on in drill sets, or whenever it's switched on. It turns itself off in ranked and cup sets.
        4. Look Around: random calls to check left, right, behind, up or the minimap, so you stop staring straight ahead.
        Everything is drawn on top of your screen or spoken. Repline never reads anything from Fortnite. Use the tunnel for training, not in tournaments. On console: keep this page open on your phone next to you for Focus Cue and Look Around.
