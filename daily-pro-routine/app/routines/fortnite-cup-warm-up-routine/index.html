Fortnite cup warm-up routine

Warm hands, calm head, nothing new. A short warm-up for cash cups, FNCS and scrims: aim, edits, freebuild, piece control, a few fights and a final cup check, so you load into the first game ready.

Start this routine free

Runs in the browser and in the Windows app with an in-game overlay.

Before a cup you don't train, you wake up. Keep it short, keep settings unchanged and stop before you get tired.

Questions

How long is this routine?
It comes in 3 lengths, from 30 min to 1 h 43 min. Pick the one that fits your day; every set has its own timer.
Do I need to pay for the map codes?
No. Every map is a free Fortnite creative map. Paste the code in Fortnite under Island Code. KovaaK's playlists need KovaaK's on Steam.
Can I run this routine with timers?
Yes. Open it in Repline: it walks you through set by set with a timer, the map code ready to copy and your playtime on the leaderboard. Free, sign in with Discord.
